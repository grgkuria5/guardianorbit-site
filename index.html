Features

Safety tools you can actually see working.

A family locator with exact coordinates, teen driving safety for rides, and alerts that stay honest about what they can and cannot do.

Live precise location

See where family members are in real time, with latitude and longitude to six decimal places, plus altitude, accuracy, and the time of the last update. During trips and SOS, GuardianOrbit uses the iPhone’s most precise GPS mode and discards stale or fuzzy readings. Tap coordinates to copy them; every SOS alert includes them and a map link.

One-tap SOS

When a teen needs help, one tap notifies the family circle with live location and coordinates. GuardianOrbit does not automatically call 911. The teen confirms before a call to 911 is placed, so emergency services are contacted on purpose — not by accident.

Teen confirms before 911

Crash and fall alerts

On Apple Watch, a sudden crash or hard fall can trigger a safety alert to the family circle, with location attached. These alerts are safety notifications only. GuardianOrbit does not diagnose injuries, provide medical advice, or act as a medical device.

Not a medical device

Abduction alert mode

When abduction-alert mode is triggered, the family circle is notified and can follow a live location trail to help first responders. This is a family alerting and tracking tool. It cannot detect every dangerous situation or guarantee an outcome.

Rideshare guard

Before a ride, the teen can enter or scan the Uber or Lyft plate. If the car leaves the expected route, parents get an alert with live location and coordinates so they can check in quickly — a teen driving safety tool for hired rides, not just family cars.

Earned privacy dial

Teens who keep check-ins on time can earn a little more privacy — a dimmer, not a blackout. Parents still have safety tools when they matter, and every sharing change stays visible to the teen.

Duress PIN

A second PIN can be set to look like a normal unlock or “I’m fine” action, while silently alerting parents. It is an extra signal for high-stress moments, not a guarantee against a determined threat.

Earthquake reunion plan

Built with the Bay Area in mind, families can pre-set a reunion place, a check-in plan, and backup contacts if networks get busy after a quake. Open it in one tap when you need it.

Safe havens

Mark trusted places — home, school, a job, a relative’s house. Family members can see arrivals and departures, and teens have a short list of agreed places to go if they need to get somewhere safe.

Privacy promise

Safety without a hidden feed.

GuardianOrbit is built so teens know what is shared, families stay in control, and Earn It Not Given LLC does not sell your data.

Teen consent

Location sharing and family-circle features require the teen’s consent. A parent cannot silently enroll a teen who has not agreed to share.

Visible sharing

The teen can see who is in the circle, what is being shared, and when it last updated — including live coordinates when precise location is on.

No data selling

We do not sell personal information. Location, account email, and emergency contacts are used to run the features you turn on, not to advertise.

Ages 13+

GuardianOrbit is for families with users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA).

FAQ

Questions parents usually ask first.

When will GuardianOrbit be available?

GuardianOrbit is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch. You can use the waitlist form on this page to see how signup will work. The form is a placeholder until launch and does not send or store your email.

Does the app automatically call 911?

No. One-tap SOS notifies the family circle with live location and coordinates. The teen confirms before a call to 911 is placed. GuardianOrbit never places an automatic 911 call.

How precise is the location?

Each family member’s card and the SOS screen show live latitude and longitude to six decimal places (about 11 centimeters, or four inches, at the equator), plus accuracy, altitude, and the time of the last update. During trips and SOS, the app uses the iPhone’s most precise GPS mode and throws out stale or fuzzy readings so parents are less likely to see a wrong spot. Accuracy still depends on GPS conditions, and no locator can promise a perfect fix in every place, such as indoors or in a tunnel.

Is this a medical app or medical device?

No. Crash and fall alerts from Apple Watch are safety notifications, not a diagnosis or treatment of any condition. GuardianOrbit is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical emergency.

What ages is GuardianOrbit for?

GuardianOrbit is designed for families with users 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Do you sell personal data?

No. Earn It Not Given LLC does not sell personal data. Location, account email, and emergency contacts are used to run the safety features you turn on. Location is shared only with the family circle the teen has consented to, and sharing stays visible in the app.

Can my teen see what is being shared?

Yes. Sharing is visible to the teen, and using the family locator features requires the teen’s consent. The earned privacy dial can reduce day-to-day visibility when check-ins stay on time, but it does not hide the fact that safety tools exist, and changes remain in view.

How much does it cost?

A Free plan is planned for getting started. Premium is planned at $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year. Family is planned at $7.99 per month for a larger household. Paid plans include a 7-day trial. Subscriptions will be billed through Apple’s App Store. Prices are in US dollars and may be refined before launch.

How do I delete my account and data?

You will be able to delete your account and associated personal data in the app. After launch, you can also reach us from the Support page. See the Privacy Policy for what we collect and how deletion works.

Will GuardianOrbit be on Android?

The first release is for iPhone and Apple Watch. We may consider other platforms later; this site does not announce an Android date.
