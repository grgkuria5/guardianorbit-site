Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Earn It Not Given LLC

This policy describes the planned privacy practices for the GuardianOrbit iPhone and Apple Watch app and this website. The app is coming soon and is not yet generally available. This page is not legal advice. Practices may be updated before launch; we will post the current policy here and, where required, in the app.

Who we are

GuardianOrbit is published by Earn It Not Given LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”), 335 Donohoe St, East Palo Alto, CA 94303, United States. For privacy questions after launch, use the Support page. The address support@guardianorbit.app is a placeholder and may not be monitored until the domain and inbox are set up.

Who the app is for (COPPA — ages 13+)

GuardianOrbit is a teen safety and family locator app for parents and teens. It is intended only for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe we have collected personal information from a child under 13, contact us through the Support page and we will delete it.

Personal information we collect

When the app launches, we plan to collect the following personal information, only as needed to provide the features you use:

Account email

We collect the email address used to create and sign in to a GuardianOrbit account, to identify the account, send transactional messages (such as security or service notices), and respond to support requests.

Location

With the user’s permission, we collect precise location so the family locator and safety features can work. This can include latitude and longitude (shown in the app to six decimal places), estimated accuracy, altitude, and the time of the reading. During trips and SOS, the app is designed to use the iPhone’s most precise GPS mode and to discard stale or low-accuracy readings. Location is used to show live position to consented family members, to attach coordinates and a map link to SOS and other safety alerts, and to power features such as safe haven arrivals, rideshare guard, and abduction-alert mode when those features are turned on. Location is shared only within the family circle the teen has consented to, except when you confirm a call to emergency services and choose to share a location with 911.

Emergency contacts

We collect the names and phone numbers of emergency contacts that a family adds in the app, so SOS and related alerts can reach the people you designate.

Information needed to operate the circle

To run the product we may also store family-circle membership, consent and sharing settings, check-in history, SOS and alert events, and in-app preferences. These records exist so the features you turn on can function and so you can review or delete them.

This marketing website does not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and the waitlist forms on this site do not send or store email addresses (they are placeholders).

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve GuardianOrbit’s safety and locator features.
  • To show precise location and coordinates to people in a consented family circle.
  • To deliver SOS and other safety alerts you configure, including alerts that a teen confirms before a 911 call is placed.
  • To create and secure accounts, and to communicate about the service.
  • To honor deletion and other privacy requests.
  • To comply with law and protect the safety of users when we have a good-faith need to do so.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising. We do not use this site to run advertising or analytics pixels.

How we share information

We share personal information only as follows:

  • Your family circle. Location and safety alerts are shared with members the teen has consented to include, and sharing is visible in the app.
  • Emergency services. GuardianOrbit does not automatically call 911. If a user confirms a 911 call, location may be shared with emergency services as part of that call or follow-up, subject to the device and carrier.
  • Service providers. We may use infrastructure providers (for example, Apple’s platforms for distribution, push notifications, or in-app purchase, and any hosting we use to operate accounts) solely to run the service. We will not authorize them to sell your data. We will list material providers in this policy when they are in use at launch.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith it is necessary to protect a person’s safety, investigate abuse, or defend our rights.
  • Business transfer. If Earn It Not Given LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, personal information may transfer under this policy or a successor policy you are notified about.

Permissions on iPhone and Apple Watch

The app will request system permissions, such as Location, only with an explanation in the system prompt and in the app. You can change permissions in iOS or watchOS Settings. If you turn location off, live locator and many safety features will not work as designed.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the service, honor your account, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Live location is meant to reflect recent, accurate readings; stale points are designed to be discarded for display. We will publish more specific retention periods at launch if they differ by data type.

Deleting your information

You will be able to delete your account and associated personal data in the app (for example, from Settings or an account-deletion control). When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify personal information we no longer need, except where we must retain a limited record to comply with law, resolve a dispute, or prevent abuse. You may also request deletion through the Support page. We will describe the exact in-app path in the shipped app and may update this paragraph to match it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to appeal a request we deny. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of “sale” or certain “sharing.” We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, use the Support page. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Security

We will use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

This website

This static website serves product information, this policy, terms, and support. It does not set advertising cookies or load third-party trackers. Waitlist forms are placeholders and do not submit your email to a server.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product develops. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice as required, such as in the app or by email to the account address.
