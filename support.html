Delete your data

Once the app ships, you will be able to delete your account and associated personal data in the app. You may also request deletion through this page after a monitored inbox is live. See the Privacy Policy for what we collect (location, account email, and emergency contacts) and how deletion works.

Billing and subscriptions

Paid plans (Premium $4.99/month or $39.99/year, and Family $7.99/month, with a 7-day trial) will be billed through the App Store. To change or cancel a subscription after purchase, use your Apple ID subscription settings. Apple handles refunds under its terms.

What to include in a support request

When a monitored inbox is live, please include your account email, whether you are writing as a parent or a teen, and a short description of the issue (account access, privacy or deletion, a bug, or a safety-feature question). Do not send passwords. For App Store charges, include the Apple transaction if you have it.

Product questions

Many common questions — including that we never automatically call 911, that crash and fall alerts are not a medical diagnosis, and that the app is for ages 13+ — are answered in the FAQ. Legal details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
